About the shop

A small halal counter on West Street.

Al Barakah is an independent halal grocer and butcher set into the run of shops on West Street, a few minutes from Boston town centre. The shop pairs a fresh meat counter with the everyday grocery shelves that families across the area rely on — flour, rice, pulses, oils, spices, frozen goods and the bits you forget to pick up at the supermarket.

The aim is simple: a clean counter, fair prices, halal-certified meat cut the way you ask for it, and a friendly hello when you walk through the door. Pop in on the way home and you'll usually find someone happy to grind, dice or trim to order.

“A proper neighbourhood shop — fresh meat at the counter, the staples on the shelves, the people you know behind the till.”

Trade has grown by word of mouth across Boston, drawing in regulars from PE21 and the surrounding villages who want halal meat from a butcher who can answer their questions, and grocery essentials in one stop.

Find the shop

Pop in to West Street.

Al Barakah is on West Street, a short walk from Boston market place. Free on-street parking is available nearby and the shop is on the bus route into town.
