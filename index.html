About the shop

A walk-in halal grocer, with the butcher’s counter at the back.

Al Barakah sits halfway down West Street, between the bus stop and the river. The shelves run Middle Eastern through North African through South Asian — rice in bags large enough for a household, the spices people actually cook with, dates by the kilo, tinned everything.

At the back the butcher works to order. Tell him what you want and how you want it. Whole birds, leg-of-lamb butterflied for the oven, beef cut for biryani, mince done while you queue.

“Al Barakah” is the Arabic word for blessing — what we hope to send home with every customer. The shop on West Street
What we sell

From the counter, and off the shelves.

The Butcher’s Counter cuts to order

  • Lamb Shoulder, leg, neck, chops. Bone-in or boned for the rolling pan.
  • Mutton Slow-cook cuts — leg, shoulder, shank — the ones that reward an afternoon.
  • Beef Shin, brisket, mince. Cubed for curry, sliced thin for the grill.
  • Chicken Whole birds, breasts off the bone, thighs with skin. Halved, quartered, jointed.
  • Mince Lamb, beef or mixed, ground while you wait. Coarse for kofta, fine for kibbeh.
  • To order Whole-lamb butchery for events and Eid — ask at the counter, allow a few days.
  • All halal · zabiha · ask for offcuts and bones for stock

On the Shelves store cupboard

  • Rice & grains Basmati in 5- and 10-kilo bags, bulgur, freekeh, vermicelli.
  • Pulses Red and green lentils, chickpeas, mung, kidney, black-eyed — dried and tinned.
  • Spices Cardamom, cumin, coriander, fenugreek, sumac, saffron, ras-el-hanout, garam masala.
  • Oils & ferments Olive, sunflower, mustard, black-seed. Tahini, pomegranate molasses, date syrup.
  • The sweet shelf Medjool and deglet dates, halva, baklava trays, rose-water, orange-blossom.
  • Fridge & freezer Paneer, feta, labneh, ready-made parathas, samosa pastry, frozen vegetables.
  • Categories change week to week — ask if you can’t see something
